How we evaluate AI tools

A useful AI comparison should explain what was checked, what remains uncertain and how a reader can make the next decision.

Updated 4 November 2026Research-based editorial guide

Start with primary sources

We check provider product pages, documentation, pricing pages and help centers. Each guide includes links to the pages used for product facts. Secondary opinions can suggest questions, but they are not a substitute for a provider’s current terms. Source links are reviewed as part of updates, not continuously monitored.

Compare a specific job

Our editorial criteria are workflow fit, access model, input and output requirements, practical limitations, and the effort needed to use an output. Category pages explain why different tools suit different work. There is no universal winner across chat, coding, creative production and research.

  • Identify the job and expected deliverable.
  • Check the product surface: consumer app, editor, terminal agent or API.
  • Check which access route exposes the relevant capability.
  • Describe a representative first task and a way to inspect the result.
  • List meaningful limitations and alternatives.

Research-based, not claimed hands-on testing

Unless a page explicitly describes a reproducible test with inputs, settings, model version, date and outputs, treat it as a research-based editorial guide. We do not claim to have run products merely because we describe a workflow. We do not award numerical accuracy, speed or creativity scores without a documented measurement method.

Pricing and availability

Prices vary with billing period, location, tax, promotion and account type. Many products also charge by seats, credits, tokens or output duration. We describe the access model and link to official pricing instead of pretending a single number is a permanent total cost. A free tier can still have strict quotas or output restrictions.

Dates, corrections and commercial relationships

The date on a guide records this content revision, not a promise that every provider remains unchanged afterward. Provider specifications are checked during revisions. Readers can request a correction through the contact page. Current outbound product links are ordinary editorial links without affiliate tracking. Any future sponsored or affiliate placement should be disclosed beside the affected link and should not be described as independent testing.
