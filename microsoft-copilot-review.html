Microsoft Copilot Review: Microsoft 365 Workflows & Plans

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft’s AI assistant product family, including assistance connected to Microsoft 365 applications. It is not GitHub Copilot, the developer-focused coding product. Consider it when your working documents, email and presentations already live in Microsoft’s ecosystem. Evaluate the exact account and license you use: consumer, business and organizational experiences can differ, and a demonstration does not establish which features your subscription includes.

Which Copilot are you comparing?

Microsoft’s official individual page describes assistance in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook with plan conditions. GitHub Copilot is a separate coding product. Before comparing prices, identify whether you need general chat, help in an Office document or organizational access to shared information. Those requirements can lead to different licenses and administrator decisions.

Test inside the application you use

Use a non-sensitive document or workbook that represents your normal work. Ask for one bounded result, such as a meeting summary or a proposed presentation outline. Check whether the tool can access the source and whether the output stays editable in the required format. Moving a result into your final workflow can matter as much as the quality of its first draft.

Verify spreadsheet and presentation output

For a workbook, inspect formulas, ranges and units against a known example. For a presentation, check the narrative, supporting evidence and speaker needs rather than counting slides. For email, verify recipients and any promise about dates or pricing. A convenient in-app assistant still needs the same substantive checks you would apply to a colleague’s first draft.

Check organization controls

Ask the administrator which account, sharing rules and AI capabilities are approved. Do not assume a personal subscription can see or safely process every workplace document. Test permissions with a normal user account before broader adoption. Choose the plan that supports the actual team workflow rather than purchasing a familiar product name.

Frequently asked questions

Is Microsoft Copilot the same as GitHub Copilot?

No. Microsoft Copilot covers general and Microsoft 365 assistance, while GitHub Copilot is focused on software development.

Does free Copilot include every Microsoft 365 feature?

No. Check the current plan comparison for application access, usage and eligible accounts.

Should I choose Copilot or ChatGPT?

Compare the same real task and the required application integration. Include editing, verification and transfer effort in the decision.
