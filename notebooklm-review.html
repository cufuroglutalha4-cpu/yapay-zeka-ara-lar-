NotebookLM Is Now Gemini Notebook: Review & Research Guide

Gemini Notebook is Google’s research and learning product formerly called NotebookLM. Google announced the rename in July 2026 while describing it as the same standalone product with expanding ecosystem connections. It is useful when the question should be answered from a defined set of materials, such as course notes, research documents or an approved project pack. The quality of that source collection matters as much as the format of the generated summary.

What happened to NotebookLM?

Google’s July 2026 announcement renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook and described continued standalone research functionality alongside closer ecosystem connections. The old name remains common in searches and tutorials. Check the date of any instructions you follow, especially when they describe plan limits or interfaces. A familiar name does not guarantee the guide reflects the current product.

Build a useful source collection

Choose materials that answer the actual research question. Label versions and remove duplicates that could confuse the evidence. Separate primary documents from commentary and note the dates that matter. A notebook containing everything you found is not necessarily better than a smaller collection of relevant, authoritative sources. Start with an explicit question and add material when a gap appears.

Turn summaries into learning

Ask for a brief explanation, then request the source behind each important point. For study, identify what you cannot yet explain in your own words and return to the relevant passage. Use generated questions to test understanding rather than rereading a polished summary repeatedly. Audio or visual formats can be convenient, but they do not replace checking the underlying material.

Handle disagreement and missing evidence

Ask the notebook to identify conflicting claims and questions the supplied material cannot answer. Inspect the cited context yourself. Keep the distinction between a source’s statement and your own conclusion clear when exporting notes. For business use, check sharing and account rules before adding internal material or publishing a notebook to a broader audience.

Frequently asked questions

Is NotebookLM now Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Google announced the rename in July 2026 and described it as the same standalone research product with expanded ecosystem connections.

Is Gemini Notebook free?

Google documents free access and higher-limit plans. Eligibility and advanced features vary by account and region.

Can a notebook answer beyond its sources?

Define the intended evidence boundary and inspect the answer. For source-grounded work, ask it to identify gaps instead of filling them with assumptions.
