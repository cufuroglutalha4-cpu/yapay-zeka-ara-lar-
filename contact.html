Contact & corrections

AI products change quickly. A specific, source-backed correction is the most useful way to help keep this library accurate.

Updated 4 November 2026Research-based editorial guide

Email ModelPanel AI

modelpanelai@gmail.com

Please include the page address and a short description of the issue. If the correction concerns pricing, tell us the region and billing period shown. A link to the provider’s documentation helps us verify it.

Useful messages

  • An outdated product name, feature or access requirement.
  • A broken official source or internal page link.
  • A missing distinction between similarly named products.
  • An accessibility or navigation issue, with device and browser details.

What to expect

This address is for editorial feedback about ModelPanel AI. It is not product billing support and cannot access your accounts with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or another provider. Contact the relevant provider directly for subscription or account problems. No response-time guarantee is offered.
