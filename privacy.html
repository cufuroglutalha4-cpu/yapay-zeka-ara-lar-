Privacy & cookie choices

The site can be browsed without an account. Optional analytics remains off until you accept it; your comparison shortlist is stored in your own browser.

Updated 4 November 2026Research-based editorial guide

Optional Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, the site loads Google Analytics using measurement ID G-6D3SDGTKHZ. It sends page visits and selected interaction events such as opening an official product link, adding a comparison tool or changing a category filter. Search text is not intentionally sent as a custom event parameter. Page addresses, including query parameters, may be included in standard page measurements; do not enter personal information into site searches.

Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled by this site’s configuration. Google may process technical information such as browser details, device information and online identifiers when analytics is enabled. See Google’s own privacy information for its handling of data.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer to accept or decline optional analytics. Declining stops new analytics events from this site and attempts to remove accessible Analytics cookies. It cannot undo information already transmitted. Clearing browser storage resets your choice. If browser storage is blocked, a preference may not persist across visits.

What is stored in your browser

  • mp-analytics-consent: your optional analytics choice.
  • mp-shortlist: up to three tools selected for comparison.

These values use localStorage. The shortlist is not sent to a ModelPanel AI account or server by the comparison feature. Shared comparison links contain the selected tool identifiers. Clearing website data removes the saved shortlist.

Hosting and external services

The site is delivered by its hosting provider, which may process connection logs needed to serve and protect the website. Links to product providers open their own sites and are subject to those providers’ privacy terms. The website does not embed third-party videos or load external fonts in this edition.

Email and privacy questions

The contact page opens your email application. Any message you send is handled through the email provider, not a form stored on this website. Do not send passwords, API keys or confidential source files. You can use the same contact address to ask about this notice.
