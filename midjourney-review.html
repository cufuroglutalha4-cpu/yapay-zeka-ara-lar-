Midjourney Review: Creative Workflows, Plans & Privacy

Midjourney is a generative creative service often considered for illustration, concept development and visual exploration. Its current plan documentation also includes video allowances, so old descriptions limited to Discord-based still images are incomplete. The main decision is whether its creative workflow and subscription structure fit the kind of assets you need. Evaluate it with a real visual brief, including privacy requirements and revision needs, rather than judging only a gallery of unusually successful outputs.

What does Midjourney suit best?

Consider it when the brief allows visual exploration: a campaign mood, illustration direction or scene concept. Write down what must stay consistent across the set, such as palette, subject identity and camera distance. Then generate candidates against that fixed brief. A beautiful image is not automatically a useful asset if it leaves no room for a headline or depicts the wrong product detail.

What the subscription changes

Midjourney’s official comparison distinguishes paid plans by usage, concurrency, Relax modes and privacy features. Video also has plan-specific allowances. Start by estimating the kind and number of usable assets you need, then leave room for rejected generations. Check the billing period and renewal terms. A lower monthly equivalent on an annual plan involves a different commitment from paying month to month.

Check privacy before the first client prompt

The plan documentation limits Stealth Mode to specified higher tiers. Do not assume every paid account makes work private. Before uploading confidential reference assets, review visibility settings and the applicable terms. For shared creative work, keep a record of authorized references and the approved direction. This also helps when a client asks why a particular result was selected over a more dramatic but less accurate alternative.

How to compare image generators fairly

Test one editorial illustration, one product-oriented scene and one requested revision. Compare consistency and time to an acceptable result, rather than asking every tool for a vague cinematic image. Include the effort needed to clean up text and prepare the final dimensions. If conversational editing or an existing Adobe workflow is your priority, compare those routes directly rather than assuming one creative service is the universal best choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Midjourney free?

Its official plan documentation lists paid subscriptions. Check current official offers rather than relying on old references to free trials.

Are Midjourney generations private?

Do not assume so. Official documentation associates Stealth Mode with specific plans; review your visibility settings before using confidential assets.

Does Midjourney only create images?

Current plan documentation includes video generation allowances as well as image usage. Availability and limits differ by plan.

Is Midjourney better than DALL-E?

For a current purchase, compare Midjourney with current OpenAI image tools. DALL·E API models are legacy products, so old comparisons need updating.
